House Walk

One floor plan (31' × 38'-3"), built as three different two-storey houses. Walk Jack through any of them on a computer or a phone. Open doors, take the stairs, look around.

Modern Cozy

Stone and teak outside, warm Italian and Turkish Diana marble, oak floors and walnut panelling inside. Flat roof with a water tank and solar panels.

Walk in ▸

Spanish Villa

White stucco, round arches, a terracotta tile roof and floors, wooden beams and wrought-iron railings.

Walk in ▸
Controls: WASD + mouse on a computer; on a phone, hold it sideways, left thumb walks and right thumb looks. Tap or press E on a door to open it.
